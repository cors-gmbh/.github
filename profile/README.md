We are a Pimcore Gold Partner from Wels, Austria. We design, build and run Pimcore platforms for industry and retail, and wire them into SAP, ERP, PLM and CRM systems. One small team covers the whole job, from architecture to production on call.

We also work on the platform itself: more than 140 merged pull requests to the Pimcore core, the first Most Valuable Pimconaut (2018), the first Pimcore Studionaut Award (2025), and the founding of [CoreShop](https://github.com/coreshop/CoreShop) in 2014.

---

### 🛠 What we build with

`Pimcore` · `Pimcore Studio` · `CoreShop` · `Symfony` · `React` · `TypeScript` · `GraphQL` · `Docker` · `Kubernetes` · `ArgoCD` · `Prometheus` · `Claude Code` · `MCP`

### ⚙️ How we work

- **Every change goes through a PR** and a second reviewer, including code an AI wrote.
- **Shared CI and quality rules** across the org, with automated dependency updates.
- **One Docker-based dev environment** for every project.
- **Infrastructure as code and GitOps** on Kubernetes.
- **AI-assisted development** with Claude Code, our own [Pimcore skills](https://github.com/cors-gmbh/pimcore-skills), and an MCP server for our project tool, Chronos.

### 🏗 What you would work on

- **Commerce at scale:** Pimcore and CoreShop shops with millions of products and orders around the clock.
- **One platform, many channels:** one product data platform that feeds many websites across several countries.
- **Integrations:** SAP, Dynamics 365 Business Central, Salesforce, HubSpot, BMEcat, ETIM/eCl@ss.
- **Pimcore Studio:** new React extensions and migrating ExtJS interfaces to Studio.
